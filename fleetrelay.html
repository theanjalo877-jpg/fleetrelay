FleetRelay explores how a passenger-transport operator could coordinate a replacement bus, an available driver and vehicle recovery from a single map.

Available for prospective business discussions

Tailored demonstrations and proposed adaptations can be discussed on request, based on a prospective client’s operational priorities.

What you are experiencing
Real Al Ain street geography with animated fictional vehicles, a rule-based assistant and simulated driver notifications. Journey time is accelerated. Refreshing clears the session.

What a production implementation needs
Authenticated driver accounts, consented GPS tracking, a production tile and road-routing service, reliable notifications, verified availability and eligibility, secure storage and operational testing. This demo does not provide emergency response or control traffic signals.

Recommendation logic checks seat capacity, availability and the next assignment. It is an explainable simulation of an AI-assisted workflow, not a connected AI model or a guarantee of a safe dispatch decision.
